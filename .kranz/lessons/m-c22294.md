In sandboxed milestone validators, `cargo test --workspace` fails with "Operation not permitted" errors from existing tests that touch their temp-dir files and git lock files. So when a contract includes that assertion, plan for it to fail at the milestone gate for reasons unrelated to the mission. Cite non-sandboxed worker test output as the evidence when triaging it, and keep it bound at the final gate.
