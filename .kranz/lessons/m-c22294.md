A `cargo test --workspace` contract assertion can fail at one milestone gate and pass at the next with no relevant change. In m-c22294 it passed at milestone 1, failed at milestone 2 and passed at the final gate. The gate kept only the tail of the error stream, and that tail did not name the failing test. The "Operation not permitted" lines in it are printed by the sandbox containment tests on every passing run, so they are not evidence of a sandbox fault. Do not waive the assertion on that reading. Keep it bound at the final gate, and rerun the command with its full output captured to find the failing test.
