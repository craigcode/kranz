Be careful with `cargo test --workspace` as a contract command assertion. The engine kills contract commands at 600 s (ticket contract-command-timeout), and the contract can't be revised after approval. On this repo the full suite takes about 5 minutes with a warm build on an idle machine, but it has run past 600 s on a cold build or while other missions were building, and that blocked two missions at the final gate. Prefer separate scoped commands such as `cargo test -p kranz-engine --lib` and `cargo test -p kranz-engine --test mission_test`. Wrap each one with the anti-vacuity grep: `out=$(... 2>&1) && printf '%s\n' "$out" | grep -qE 'test result: ok\. [1-9][0-9]* passed'`.
