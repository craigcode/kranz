A "no unrelated surfaces" contract command that diffs against `$KRANZ_BASE_SHA` must exclude Kranz's own bookkeeping paths, or it fails on every mission: the mandatory `[kranz] approved plan` commit sits between the pinned base and HEAD and always writes `.kranz/missions/index.md`, `.kranz/missions/<id>/plan.json`, and `.kranz/missions/<id>/plan.md`, and the Slack bridge writes `.kranz/slack-threads.json` during the run. Either add `^\.kranz/` to the grep exclusion (e.g. `grep -vE '^(\.kranz/|<real allow-list>)'`) or scope the diff to the milestone's own commit range instead of the pinned base.
