A negative env assertion cost a full respawn here, and the trap is durable and repo-specific: the engine's final gate now exports contract-command env (KRANZ_BASE_SHA) into subprocesses via `.envs()`, which overlays onto — never clears — the inherited environment. That's worth carrying forward.

LESSON: When testing that an env var is ABSENT from a contract-command context (e.g. KRANZ_BASE_SHA when base_sha is None), assert directly on the env-map constructor (`contract_env(None)` lacks the key), never by spawning a subprocess that checks `$VAR`. `run_shell_command`/backend `.envs()` overlays onto the inherited process env without clearing it, and the engine's own final gate exports the pinned KRANZ_BASE_SHA into every contract-command subprocess — so a subprocess-based absence test passes locally but false-CRITICALs at the real gate depending on ambient env.
