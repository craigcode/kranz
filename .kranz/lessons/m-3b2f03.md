A durable, repo-specific trap cost a full respawn cycle on both f-2-2 and f-2-3: extending a widely-used type in `kranz-slack` breaks scattered sibling sites that workers miss.

`When a kranz-slack feature adds a field to a Block Kit struct (e.g. format::Complete) or a variant to the Action enum, the worker must grep for and update EVERY construction/match site before reporting — these are split across src AND the integration-test files (tests/formatting.rs, tests/tickets.rs, tests/routing.rs), and Action has TWO exhaustive no-wildcard matches in bridge.rs (the async dispatch ~line 881 and the sync fallback apply_action ~line 1958). Spec such features to require `cargo test -p kranz-slack` COMPILES (not just the touched file) as the completion gate; both f-2-2 (missing diff_stat in test constructors) and f-2-3 (missing Action::Merge in the second match) lost a respawn to a missed sibling site.`
