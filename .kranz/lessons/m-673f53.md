NONE is tempting since much of this mission was routine, but one trap here was genuinely non-obvious, repo-specific, and cost a full respawn — worth carrying forward.

**Lesson:** In `crates/slack`, `Action::ApproveMission` (`/kranz approve <arg>`) is OVERLOADED — the bridge's `dispatch_action` (~bridge.rs:1096) routes a ticket-slug arg through the ticket-queue gate (`deps::approve_ticket`) but a mission-id arg through the plan-approval SPEND gate (`approve_flow`), disambiguated only at dispatch by `is_ticket_slug`. When adding or renaming any Slack ticket/plan verb (e.g. a D-A "Queue" verb), do NOT alias it onto `ApproveMission` — that lets the new verb trigger the sacred plan-approval spend gate. Give ticket-queueing its own `Action` variant that only ever calls the ticket gate, and write the contract assertion so `/kranz queue <mission-id>` provably cannot approve a plan. (A single `route_slash` cannot tell slug from id — that split lives in the bridge, not the parser.)
