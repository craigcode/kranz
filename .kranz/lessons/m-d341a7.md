Milestone validation in this repo runs the ENTIRE mission contract at every milestone gate, not just the assertions that milestone can satisfy — so in a multi-milestone plan that builds one deliverable incrementally, every assertion about later-milestone content (section counts, stitch pointers, cross-file greps) comes back as a "critical" finding at earlier gates and must be triaged and waived one by one. When planning such a mission, either write each contract command so the first milestone that touches its subject can already pass it (e.g. gate on "section exists OR is an explicit placeholder"), or scope the plan to one milestone per cumulative deliverable; otherwise budget a waive round per early milestone and never convert those findings into fix-features that duplicate pending features.
