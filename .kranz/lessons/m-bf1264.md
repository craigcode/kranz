NONE would undersell a genuinely non-obvious, repo-specific trap this mission hit twice (out-of-contract-write finding, waived across two rounds, plus a straggler fix): full-mission integration tests live in more than one crate, and an engine-level completion/gate change breaks the ones outside `crates/engine/tests/`.

Lesson:

When a mission changes engine mission-completion or `final_gate()` behavior (e.g. a new gate that Fails some missions), scope the touchSet AND the test-migration work to **all** full-mission integration tests across the workspace — not just `crates/engine/tests/`. In particular `crates/server/tests/host_test.rs` drives real missions through `final_gate()` with a non-delivering mock `worker_pass()` fixture, so it (and any other cross-crate full-mission test) will break under a stricter gate and must be migrated too; include `crates/server/tests/**` in the touchSet up front to avoid an out-of-contract-write finding on a change that is actually required to keep `cargo test --workspace` green.
